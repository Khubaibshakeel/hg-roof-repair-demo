About HG Roof Repair

Hugo Guerra fixes roofs. He doesn’t sell new ones. HG Roof Repair is a locally owned, repair-only roofer in San Antonio, so when a repair will do the job, a repair is what you get.

Hugo comes out, finds the leak or the damage, gives you a free estimate on site and fixes it, often the same day. Shingle, clay tile, flat and metal roofs, for homes and businesses.
